Good to know

  • About 7,700 kcal equals 1 kg of body fat. Eating 500 kcal less per day is roughly 0.5 kg per week.
  • Your weight jumps up and down by 1–2 kg day to day from water and salt. Look at the 7-day line, not single days.
  • Fried food, coconut milk (santan), sweet drinks and oil add up fast. They are the easiest places to cut.
  • The numbers in the food list are estimates. Being roughly right every day matters more than being exact.
